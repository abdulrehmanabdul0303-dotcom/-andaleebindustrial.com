About Andaleeb Industrial Company

Andaleeb Industrial Company is a fast-growing and professionally managed industrial refrigeration and cooling services provider based in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

Established in 2021, the company has carved a strong presence in the field of refrigeration systems, cold rooms, and temperature-controlled solutions, catering to businesses where uninterrupted cold-chain operations are critical.

We deliver reliable maintenance, repair, and emergency support services with skilled technicians, strong safety practices, and a customer-first approach.

Industrial Capabilities & Infrastructure

Our state-of-the-art workshop facilities and advanced diagnostic equipment enable us to handle complex industrial refrigeration challenges across the Kingdom. From precision laser-guided fabrication to heavy-duty welding, we maintain the highest standards.
